4. Delivery Charges

Delivery charges may apply to orders. The current delivery rate is ₹30 per kilometre. The website calculates an estimated driving distance from the shop location to the customer address and uses that distance to calculate the delivery charge.

5. Maps and Routing Services

To calculate delivery distance, the website may use OpenStreetMap data, Nominatim geocoding and OSRM routing services. Address information entered for delivery-distance calculation is sent to these services for geocoding/routing. Their respective usage policies apply.

6. Payments

If online payment facilities are introduced, payments may be processed by third-party payment providers. We do not intend to store complete card numbers, UPI PINs or banking passwords on this website.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

The website may use browser local storage to save cart items and order information on the customer's device.

8. Third-Party Services

The website may use third-party services for hosting, maps, routing, communication or other business functions. Third-party services may process information according to their own policies.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Retention

Information may be retained as reasonably necessary for orders, business records, dispute resolution and legal requirements.
